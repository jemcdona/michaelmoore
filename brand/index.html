Brand and media kit

Logo, colour, type, voice and photography for Lux Cut by Michael. Version 1.0, November 2026.

1 · Logo

The crown and scissors

One mark, two versions. Use the black version on dark backgrounds and photography. Use the white version on light paper, print and documents.

Primary: black

Dark backgrounds, website hero, social.

Download

Alternate: white

Light paper, invoices, print.

Download

Do

  • Keep clear space equal to the height of the crown's top ornament on every side.
  • Minimum size: 120 px wide on screen, 30 mm in print.
  • Place it on plain onyx, or on calm areas of a photo.

Don't

  • Stretch, rotate or recolour the mark.
  • Add shadows, outlines or effects.
  • Put the black version on a light background, or the white version on a dark one.

5 · Photography

The Lux look

Warm marble, brushed gold and leather. Keep photos bright and natural, with gold and espresso tones. Crop portraits to 4:5. Place text over the calm wall areas in the wide banners, never over faces.
