About Lux Cut

Meet Michael

Hi, I’m Michael Moore, an Ann Arbor native and second-generation hairstylist, behind the chair since 2008. I trained at Douglas J Aveda Institute in Ann Arbor, and today I run Lux Cut, my own studio inside Salon Varga in Saline, Michigan.

I specialize in cut and colour, creating customized looks that bring out each client’s natural style. I’m passionate about healthy hair, and I’ll always walk you through how to keep it looking and feeling its best.

Hairdressing runs in my family. My mom has been a talented stylist for over 50 years, and I’m honored to continue her legacy behind the chair.

Lux Cut is built around one idea: you should leave looking and feeling like royalty. Expect a proper consultation, precise cutting and a finish that works every day, not just in the chair.
